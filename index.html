Support

Review a scanned label

Check each scanned name, strength, and direction against the original label before saving. Edit any field that needs correcting. Choose reminder times using the schedule from your prescription label or healthcare professional.

Add a photo or change a medication

Use Add Medication Photo to photograph the pill or its package. Use Scan Medication Label to read printed text. Tap a medication in your list to edit its dosage, directions, or other details. Delete Medication removes the entry and its reminders and keeps previously recorded doses in your history.

Manage reminders

Enable notifications for MedReminder IQ in iPhone Settings. Focus and Silent mode can affect alerts. Use Take Now after taking a dose to record it. For questions about your medication or a missed dose, follow the instructions from your pharmacist or healthcare professional.

Track pill counts

In Premium Refills, tap a medication to enter how many pills you have and how many you use per dose. Logging a dose as taken reduces the estimate while Premium is active. Update the count after a refill or when it differs from your actual supply. You can set a low-stock notification threshold.

Restore Premium

Open Premium and choose Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until canceled. Manage or cancel a subscription in Apple Account settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Apple processes payments and refund requests.
