MedReminder IQ

Privacy Policy

Medication and profile information

MedReminder IQ stores the medication details, optional patient name, reminder settings, pill counts, medication photos, edited label text, and dose history you save. It also stores your chosen display name, profile photo, and preferences. Label photos and recognized text are processed on the device and used during scan review. Saved medication details, optional medication photos, and edits to the scanned text become part of your records.

Sharing and purchases

MedReminder IQ sends no medication records, scan photos, or profile data to the developer. It uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking services. Apple processes in-app purchases and supplies the purchase verification used to unlock Premium.

Apple Privacy Policy

Retention and choices

Saved records stay on your iPhone until you remove them or delete the app. Deleting a medication keeps its dose history. You can remove your profile photo in the app. Deleting the app removes its local records from this installation. Offloading keeps its data. Copies can remain in device backups or your photo library, which you can manage separately.

You can turn off camera access and notifications in iPhone Settings. The photo picker gives the app access to the images you choose. You can remove a medication photo by editing the medication.
