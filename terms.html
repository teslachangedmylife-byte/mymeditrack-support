Premium and billing

One medication and one scheduled daily reminder time are included. All Premium plans add unlimited medications, additional daily reminder times, refill and low-stock alerts, pill counts, and progress reports. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Apple displays the current price and handles billing. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings or use Restore Purchases in the app.

Using the app

My MediTrack helps you record medication details, set reminders, and log doses. Check scanned text against the original label and follow the directions from your pharmacist or healthcare professional.
